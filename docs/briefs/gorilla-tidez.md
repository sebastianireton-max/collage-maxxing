# Gorilla Tidez — site brief

Read from `instagram.com/gorillatidez` on 2026-09-09. 59 posts, 255 followers.
Everything below is what the account actually shows, not invention.

## Who they are

**Gorilla Tidez** 🦍🌊 — "Built different. **Dominate Your Limits.**"
Bio line: *Recovery • Fat Loss • Performance*.

Peptide education and motivation. Not a storefront in the posts seen — the
account sells a mindset first and positions itself as the honest voice in a
hyped category.

## The two themes that are actually the brand

**Faith.** "PRAYERS WORK, AND TABLES EVENTUALLY TURN." · "HE WALKS WITH ME —
I FEAR NO EVIL." · "BOTH FEAR AND FAITH ASK YOU TO BELIEVE IN SOMETHING YOU
CAN'T SEE. YOU CHOOSE." · "PUSH ME LORD". Crosses appear in several frames.
This is not decoration; it runs through the account and belongs on the site.

**Discipline over shortcuts.** "THERE IS NO PEPTIDE FOR DISCIPLINE." ·
"YOU CAN'T SKIP THE HARD PART." · "EDUCATION > HYPE — KNOWLEDGE BUILDS
CHAMPIONS."

That second theme is a gift, compliance-wise. A brand whose own hero line is
*there is no peptide for discipline* has already rejected the miracle-cure
framing that gets peptide sites into trouble. Build on it.

## Their own best compliance content

One post already does the work, and its framing should anchor the site:

> naturally in the human body ✅ compounds studied in biomedical research
> ✅ potential for future therapies
> **EDUCATION > HYPE**

Accurate, defensible, and on-brand. That is the site's spine.

## The compliance conflict — flag before building

Two things in the current account **cannot ship as-is** on a compliant site:

1. **"Recovery • Fat Loss • Performance"** is a human-benefit claim triad. As a
   headline for peptides it markets unapproved drugs for body composition and
   therapeutic effect. It is the single highest-risk string in the brand.
2. **"SUBQ vs IM peptide injections — does the injection route actually
   matter?"** is administration guidance. Route-of-injection content is dosing
   guidance for human use however it is framed.

Neither means the site is impossible. It means the *positioning* moves from
promising outcomes to **teaching the science honestly** — which is where the
account's own strongest content already sits. Keep the aggression, keep the
faith, keep "Dominate Your Limits"; drop the outcome triad and the route
guidance.

**Owner decision required:** whether the site keeps "Recovery • Fat Loss •
Performance" (not compliant) or leads with the education/discipline positioning
(compliant, and stronger). Do not resolve this by quietly softening the wording.

## Rules for the build

- Research framing only. No human-use claims, no dosing, no protocols, no
  stacks, no route-of-administration guidance, no before/after.
- Never state or imply a peptide treats, cures, prevents or heals anything.
- Carry the research-use disclaimer plainly, not buried.
- **Invent nothing** — no fabricated reviews, testimonials, follower counts,
  results, certifications or "trusted by" logos. The account has 255 followers;
  any social-proof number on the site would be a lie.
- Cite real studies where the science is described, or describe it generically.
  Never attribute a claim to research that was not read.
